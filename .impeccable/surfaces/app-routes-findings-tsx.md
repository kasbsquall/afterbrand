## Direction contract

THESIS: The whole library is visible as a core-sample strip, one column per page and one layer per finding coloured by decision, while one finding at a time is decided at reading scale. It refuses the endless grey linter list with no sense of the total.

OWN-WORLD: Deep mineral teal ground that floods the shell (oklch 22% .035 200), raised layers tinted from the same hue, never black shadows. Minerals as state colours: amber for Change, mineral green for Keep, coral for Needs you, cool grey for undecided. Hanken Grotesk at reading scale, tabular figures, 12 px corners on every surface, Phosphor Light icons.

STORY: A content lead sees how much of the rename is left, trusts that the agent read each sentence (a reason on every finding), decides the hard ones by keyboard, selects groups and asks the agent about them, and publishes only what they accepted.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar with product, two rule chips and "541 of 996 findings decided". Left 62%: the focused finding at 28 px with the old name highlighted, the proposal beneath, decision and reason, Accept, Keep, Edit with keys A, K, E; under it the selectable list with the bulk bar. Right 38%, sticky: "455 left" at 76 px, the core-sample strip, then the agent panel reading the selection.

FORM: Core sample, a foreign form from geology, candidate C of three; the seed assigned candidate 7 of the grounded list (Errata slip), which the user saw and declined. Seed key e1eb9a23.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
